# 🎮 NITRO RUSH — How to Play

Welcome to **NITRO RUSH**, an arcade-style endless car racing game! Dodge traffic, control your speed, and beat your personal high score.

## 🏁 1. Start the Race
- Click **START RACE** to begin.
- Wait for the countdown: 3, 2, 1, GO!
- Your car starts moving automatically.

## 🕹️ 2. Game Controls

**Desktop / Laptop**
- ⬅️ **Left Arrow / A** — Steer left.
- ➡️ **Right Arrow / D** — Steer right.
- ⬆️ **Up Arrow / W** — Accelerate.
- ⬇️ **Down Arrow / S** — Brake and slow down.
- **Spacebar** — Pause or resume the race.
- **Esc** — Pause the race or return to the menu.

**Mobile / Touchscreen**
- ◀️ **Left button** — Move left.
- ▶️ **Right button** — Move right.
- ▲ **Accelerate button** — Increase speed.
- ▼ **Brake button** — Reduce speed.
- You can also swipe left or right on the racing area to steer.

## 🚗 3. Your Mission
- Avoid crashing into enemy cars.
- Stay on the road and avoid the edges.
- Drive faster to earn more points.
- Dodge traffic to earn bonus points for near misses.
- Survive as long as possible and cover more distance.
- Beat your previous high score!

## 📊 4. Understand the HUD
- **SCORE:** Your current points.
- **DISTANCE:** How far you have travelled.
- **BEST:** Your highest score saved on this browser.
- **KM/H:** Your current speed.

## ⚙️ 5. Customize Your Experience
- **GARAGE:** Choose between three different racing cars.
- **SETTINGS:** Enable or disable sound and motion effects.
- **DIFFICULTY:** Select Chill, Arcade, or Insane mode.
- **PAUSE:** Take a break whenever you need one.
- **RACE AGAIN:** Restart after a crash.

## 🏆 Pro Tips
1. Use short, controlled steering movements instead of holding a direction for too long.
2. Brake when traffic gets crowded.
3. Accelerate on clear sections of the road.
4. Watch distant traffic and plan your next move early.
5. Try different cars to find the handling that suits your driving style.
